Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and a password (stored in hashed form). As you use Spendly we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to authenticate you, to store and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and is accessible only to your account. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages are loaded. We do not otherwise share your expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
